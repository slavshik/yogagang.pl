# yoga gang

[yogagang.pl](https://yogagang.pl/)

Body. Breath. Community.

A yoga community in Warsaw, practising together since 2023. Yoga gang brings people together
through classes in Warsaw and online, one-to-one sessions, and retreats in the mountains.

Our focus is the body: learning to feel, move, and inhabit yourself more fully, alongside
others on the same path.

Whether you’ve been here from the start or you’ve just found us, you belong here.
