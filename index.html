body. breath. community.

a yoga community in warsaw. offline and online.

We started in 2023 with a simple belief: that movement is more powerful when shared.

Our focus is the body. Not the perfect pose, not the performance, but the real, honest work of learning to feel, move, and inhabit yourself more fully. And doing that alongside others who are on the same path.

Not a workout. Not repetition. Return.

What kept people coming back wasn’t just the yoga. It was each other.
